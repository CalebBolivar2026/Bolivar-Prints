Material Guide

Engineered for Performance & Aesthetics

We select premium filaments calibrated specifically for our Bambu Lab setup, ensuring top-tier surface finish, layer bonding, and structural strength.

Best for Decor & Organizers

PLA & PLA+

Rigid, eco-friendly, and capable of capturing exceptionally crisp details. Ideal for desk organizers, miniatures, and display pieces.

Finish: Matte / Glossy Rigidity: High
Best for Mechanical Parts

PETG

Tough, impact-resistant, and heat-resistant. Perfect for functional brackets, structural mounts, clips, and everyday utility.

Finish: Semi-Gloss Durability: Extreme
Best for Eye-Catching Display

Silk & Multi-Color

Stunning dual-tone or metallic sheen finishes that catch light from every angle. Excellent for articulated dragons and statement gifts.

Finish: Metallic / Shimmer Aesthetics: Premium
Best for Outdoor & Heat

ABS & ASA

Superior UV and high-temperature resistance. Engineered to withstand outdoor elements and rigorous industrial environments.

Finish: Matte UV Stable Temp Resistance: High
Best for Flexible Uses

TPU (Flexible)

Rubber-like elasticity and vibration dampening. Ideal for protective phone cases, custom gaskets, wheels, and shock-absorbing feet.

Finish: Rubberized Flexibility: High
Engineering Grade

Carbon Fiber & Nylon

Industrial composites reinforced with carbon fibers. Offers extreme rigidity-to-weight ratio for heavy-duty structural prototypes.

Finish: Textured Matte Strength: Industrial

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, file requirements, materials, and shipping.

Q What file formats do you accept for custom orders?

We primarily accept .STL, .STEP, or direct links to repositories like Printables and Thingiverse. If you have another file format or just a rough idea/drawing, feel free to reach out through our custom order form and we'll see how we can help!

Q What is your typical turnaround time?

Ready-to-ship store items typically ship out within 1 – 2 business days. Custom orders and prints generally have a turnaround time of 2 to 5 business days depending on print complexity, size, and current print queue volume.

Q Where are you located and do you ship?

Bolivar Prints operates directly out of Central Florida. We offer secure shipping straight to your door across the United States.

Q How do I choose between PLA and PETG?

Choose PLA/PLA+ for crisp aesthetics, desk organizers, miniatures, and decorative items. Choose PETG if your print needs to handle mechanical stress, temperature resistance, or heavy daily wear-and-tear. Check our Material Guide above for full details!
